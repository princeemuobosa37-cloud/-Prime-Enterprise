Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Inquiries

Everything you need to know about purchasing, shipping, and returns.

How can I place an order? +

Browse our catalogue on the Shop page, select your desired product, choose finish options, and click "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now". You can then proceed through our streamlined checkout flow to review order details.

Which payment methods are supported? +

Prime Enterprise supports secure Direct Bank Transfers, verified Debit/Credit cards, and Pay-on-Delivery for eligible regional delivery zones. In this current website demonstration, payment fields are presented for visual interface verification without processing real debit transactions.

How can I contact customer support? +

You can reach out directly via telephone at 08103719799 or email us at princeemuobosa37@gmail.com. We take pride in personalized, attentive communication.

What is your return policy? +

We offer a 7-day inspection and return window from the day of confirmed delivery. Items must be returned in unused condition with all original packaging, seals, warranty documentation, and accessories intact.

How can I find out about delivery? +

Once an order is confirmed, dispatch takes place within 24 to 48 business hours. Deliveries within Lagos typically arrive within 1–2 business days, while nationwide deliveries across Nigeria require 3–5 business days. Complimentary delivery is provided on orders exceeding ₦150,000.
